Breach Night

Mixed-reality home defence. Boarded doorways open in your real walls and the dead come through them. Every weapon is available from the first second.

Checking for a headset…

  • Room: the game uses your headset's room scan to find your walls. If you have never scanned this room, the headset will offer to do it.
  • Shoot: trigger. Each hand carries its own weapon. Switch: flick the thumbstick left or right. Reload: A / B / X / Y, or just run the magazine dry.
  • Barricades: zombies have to smash the planks first. Hold the grip button next to a doorway to hammer planks back on.
  • Headshots do 2.5× damage. Kill chains multiply your score. Health comes back if you stay untouched for a few seconds.
  • Desktop preview: mouse to aim, click to fire, wheel or 1–0 to switch, R reload, F repair.
  • Clear the floor around you and keep the headset's boundary on — you will be turning and swinging.
