Breach Night

Mixed-reality zombie survival in your own room. The dead come through your real doors and windows; every kill earns points to spend on the guns, perks and upgrades hanging on your walls.

Checking for a headset…

Controls

ButtonWhat it does
TriggerFire. Select in menus.
A (right) / X (left)Reload that hand's gun. Pulling the trigger on an empty gun also reloads.
B (right) / Y (left)Next weapon in that hand. Flicking the thumbstick left or right does the same.
GripBuy the wall item you are pointing at. Hold it next to a barricade to hammer boards back on.
Thumbstick clickPause menu (resume, tutorial, quit).
SwingThe machete and the Plasma Edge need a real swing, not a trigger pull.
  • Room: the game reads your headset's room scan. Zombies only enter through the doors and windows marked in it; the menu inside the headset tells you what it found. If none are marked, add them in the headset's Space Setup and press Rescan Room.
  • Points: +10 a hit, +60 a kill, +100 a headshot, +130 with a blade. You start with a pistol, a machete and 500 points.
  • On your walls: weapon buys, the Mystery Crate (950), the Overclock bench (5,000 then 10,000) and five perks.
  • Desktop preview: mouse to aim, click to fire, wheel or Q/E to switch, R reload, F buy/repair, P pause.
  • Clear the floor around you and keep the headset's boundary on — you will be turning and swinging.
