Golf Range Tools

A swing tempo trainer and a putting-line practice green. Hold one controller like a club grip, pointing down at the ball.

Checking for a headset…

  • Start: face an open bit of floor. The ball appears just in front of you and the target is to your left (right for left-handers).
  • Menu: point either controller at the floating panel and pull the trigger.
  • Fit the club: set up to the ball and press A / X — the club length snaps to your floor.
  • Tempo: hold still behind the ball until the green ring appears, then swing. You get backswing : downswing ratio (3 : 1 is the goal), frames, club speed and an estimated carry.
  • Putting: choose distance, break, slope and green speed. The white line is the ideal roll, the blue line is your start line. Every putt is scored on start line and pace.
  • Swing a controller only — never a real club — and check you have room.
